## Prosedur Pencatatan Assignment & Lab

Setiap kali menyelesaikan praktikum/assignment pada salah satu kursus di atas:
1. Simpan skrip lab di subdirektori track terkait (misal `02_intermediate_systems/ACAD-I02_vllm/`).
2. Rekam metrik hasil uji ke log terpusat:
   ```powershell
   python "C:\Antigravity\AMD_AI_Developer_Program\scripts\log_experiment.py" record `
     --track "academy_ACAD-I02" `
     --hardware-tier "Cloud_MI300X" `
     --device-name "AMD Instinct MI300X 192GB" `
     --execution-provider "vLLM_ROCm" `
     --model-or-workload "DeepSeek-R1-Distill" `
     --precision "BF16" `
     --throughput-val 120.5 `
     --throughput-unit "tokens/s" `
     --status "PASS" `
     --notes "Course ACAD-I02 lab verification"
   ```
3. Salin `experiment_id` yang dikembalikan ke kolom `Experiment Log ID` pada tabel di atas dan perbarui `Status` menjadi `COMPLETED`.
